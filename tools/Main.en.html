Our solutions

IFRS, carbon accounting and ESG solutions for Thai companies

At LO-CARB we provide one stop service in all sustainability dimension from climate defense, reporting, capacity building to business solutions.

  1. 01

    IFRS S1 & S2 consulting

    End-to-end IFRS S1/S2 support, from gap analysis and climate risk and financial impact assessment to your first disclosure.

    ServiceISSB disclosureExplore IFRS advisory
  2. 02

    CarbonCal carbon accounting platform

    Measure Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions on one easy, accurate platform, so your carbon footprint for organization (CFO) is ready for disclosure and TGO registration, with training that brings your team on board.

    PlatformCFO · TGOTry CarbonCal free
  3. 03

    ISSB training for boards and teams

    Hands-on ISSB reporting training from Thailand's first ISSB training partner.

    Training1st ISSB training partnerSee training options
  4. 04

    Carbon Scout: farmland carbon tracking

    Track and verify carbon on farmland and plantations, ready for carbon credits.

    PlatformCarbon creditsBook a demo
  5. 05

    Sustainability-related reporting & consulting

    Sustainability and ESG reports and strategy that meet GRI, CDP and other standards, through to your Net Zero pathway, led by our specialist team.

    ServiceGRI · CDP · Net ZeroExplore reporting
